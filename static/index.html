Table 1: Pricing Benchmarks & Task Category Breakdown i Pricing Benchmark Criteria & Methodology
  • Hourly vs. Flat Fee Split: Hourly labor rates ($/hr) and fixed project budgets (Flat $) are calculated in separate buckets so hourly and flat-rate jobs are never mixed.
  • Employee Wages Excluded: Employer Job Postings (companies hiring hourly helpers/workers) are excluded so benchmarks reflect true Customer & Contractor service pricing.
  • Price-Bypass & Outlier Cutoffs:
    • Hourly Rate: Counts $25/hr – $250/hr (ignores $1–$20 classified placeholders).
    • Flat Fee: Counts $45 – $5,000 (ignores $1–$40 classified bypass numbers, $123/$999 dummy values, and cheap hardware store part mentions).
